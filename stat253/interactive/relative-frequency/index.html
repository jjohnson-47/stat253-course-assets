What to watch

Start with 10 trials and notice the noise. Then add 100 and 1,000. The proportion does not become exactly equal to the theoretical probability, but its swings become smaller. Try a biased coin last: relative frequency converges there too, just to a different target.

The takeaway

Relative frequency is an estimate made from data. More trials do not force the estimate to equal the true probability, but they make a large miss less common. That remains true when the probability is not one-half and symmetry cannot tell you its value.
